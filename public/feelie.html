Stuff I'm Never Throwing Out

(by Dave. Do NOT touch. Mom, this means you.)

Things I figured out the hard way

  • A pencil is a tool for fixing tapes. Writing is its side job.
  • If a game won't start, blow on it. Don't ask me why that works.
  • Beepers: somebody wants you to call them back. Call them back.
  • The best hiding spot is under the thing nobody ever lifts.
  • Rewind before you return it. (Late fees. Never again.)
  • Some songs sound like nonsense until you play them the other way.
  • Saturday morning is the best time to find anything.

How to use the house

Type short. LOOK a lot. EXAMINE everything. Open what opens. If you're stuck, type HINT. There is no shame in a hint. I used the back of the game box.

What I couldn't say out loud

You don't have to like any of it. I just wanted you to know where I came from. Come find me anyway.

Dad

P.S. Don't lose the pencil.
